About the numbers

Traffic figures are rough public estimates, not measured data — treat them as an order of magnitude, not a scoreboard. Small sites also swing hard month to month: one viral post can triple a shop's visits for a week. If a number here looks wrong to you, it probably is, and the form above is the place to say so.
