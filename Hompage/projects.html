Remebering Georgetown

For my project I decided to allow users to explore the neighborhood Georgetown in Washington D.C. and how it’s been gentrified. I am originally from Silver Spring, Maryland and was born in Georgetown and worked there during my year off from school so making this website was pretty personal to me. Georgetown is considered one of the first neighborhoods in D.C. to be gentrified, with a Black population of 30-40% in the 1930s over time the demographic of the area changed and now Georgetown's Black population is less than 8%. Gentrification not only displaces people as housing costs rise and buildings get torn down but it also erases culture and that is what I wanted to explore in my project. Schools, businesses, and housing developments are displayed on the website and I think it’s just always important to acknowledge who's been in an area before you and what stood in some of the places you frequent today.
